ABOUT THE ARCHIVE / 02

A living record of where we come from.

This bloodline was collected by our ancestors and preserved through generations. In the 20th century, the records were gathered and translated by the late Hazi Ahkam Uddin. In the 21st century, Hasibur Rahman brought the record into a digital form and continued adding information beyond the 25th generation.

The tree is an evolving archive: a place to remember names, honour the people before us, and help the next generation find their way back.
