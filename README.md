# Family Tree

An interactive digital archive of our family lineage, preserving names and ancestry across generations.

## About the archive

The original family information was collected by our ancestors. During the 20th century, the records were gathered and translated by the late Hazi Ahkam Uddin. In the 21st century, Hasibur Rahman brought the record into digital form and continued adding information beyond the 25th generation.

Scanned source materials are available in the [image archive](image.html).

## Features

- Interactive top-down family tree powered by [D3.js](https://d3js.org/)
- Expand and collapse individual branches
- Expand or collapse the complete tree
- Pan and zoom around large family branches
- Switch names between Bangla, English, and Arabic
- Right-to-left rendering for Arabic names
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile screens
